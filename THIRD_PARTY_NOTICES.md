# Third-party components

The original PongFetch design is covered by LICENSE.md. Third-party components retain their own licenses and are not relicensed by PongFetch.

- **BOSL2** — https://github.com/BelfrySCAD/BOSL2 — BSD-2-Clause. Required by the SCAD design; install separately. Keep its copyright and license notices when redistributing the library. The library is not included in the design-source ZIP.
- **OpenSCAD** — https://openscad.org — design tool, installed separately; not included in the download.
- **Arial Bold** — used by `Branding.scad`. Install a properly licensed local font or change `brand_font` to a font available to you. Font files are not included. Recheck geometry and text fit after changing the font.
- **Bambu Studio profiles** — the 3MF projects retain saved machine and material settings. The PongFetch design license applies to original PongFetch design content, not third-party software, profile content or trademarks.
